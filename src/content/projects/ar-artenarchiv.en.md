## The idea

AR-Artenarchiv makes species extinction tangible through a spatial experience. Five extinct or possibly extinct animal species appear in the real environment. Information panels complement the encounter with facts about the species and the threats they face.

## My contribution

In our two-person team, I handled the entire implementation, including the UI and interaction. This covered animal selection, placement through hand tracking, and the integration of room data and spatial anchors. My project partner handled the models and animations, which I integrated into the application.

## Implementation

The application uses Godot and OpenXR on Meta Quest 3. Passthrough keeps the real environment visible while virtual animals are placed within it. One species takes centre stage at a time, from a life-sized rhinoceros to a small golden toad on the hand.

## Challenges

Hand tracking and spatial interaction had to be tested directly in the headset. Wireless remote deployment later made testing easier. For a future project, we would adopt this workflow earlier and allow more time to coordinate models, animations, and interaction.
